# How to Put Harmony Music Club Online (Free)

Your website is finished! It's made of these files:

- `index.html` — Home page
- `about.html` — About Us page
- `events.html` — Events page (with registration form)
- `contact.html` — Contact page (form + social links)
- `styles.css` — the design/colors for every page
- `script.js` — makes the menu and forms work

**Keep all files in the same folder** so the pages can find each other.

## See it on your own computer first
Double-click `index.html` — it opens in your web browser. Click around the menu to test all four pages. This only works on your computer though, so to let *everyone* see it, publish it online using one of the free options below.

## Option 1 — Netlify Drop (easiest, ~2 minutes)
Best if you just want a public link fast.

1. Go to **https://app.netlify.com/drop**
2. Drag the whole project folder onto the page.
3. Netlify gives you a public link like `your-club-name.netlify.app` — share it with anyone!
4. (Optional) Make a free account to keep the site and rename the link.

## Option 2 — GitHub Pages (permanent, great for school)
Best if you want a lasting site and don't mind a few more steps.

1. Make a free account at **https://github.com**
2. Create a new **repository** (name it something like `music-club`).
3. Upload all the files in this folder.
4. Go to **Settings → Pages**, set the source to your `main` branch, and save.
5. In a minute your site is live at `https://yourusername.github.io/music-club/`

## Things you might want to change
- **Social links:** On the Contact page, replace the `#` links with your real Instagram/TikTok/etc. URLs.
- **Email address:** Search the files for `harmonymusicclub@email.com` and swap in your real one.
- **Event dates:** Update them on `events.html` as plans change.

> Note: The registration and contact forms are demo (mockup) forms — they show a confirmation message but don't actually send data anywhere. To collect real responses for free, you can connect a free form service like **Formspree** or **Google Forms** later.
