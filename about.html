About Us

Meet the club that's all about making, sharing, and celebrating music together.

Who We Are

What We Do

The Harmony Music Club is a student-run group for anyone who loves music. We meet every week to play together, learn new skills, and put on events for our school and community.

🎧

Jam & Practice

Weekly jam sessions and practice time where members play together, try new instruments, and form bands.

📚

Learn & Grow

Workshops on songwriting, recording, music theory, and performance — taught by members and guests.

🎪

Perform Live

Showcases, open mics, and community gigs that give every member a chance to step on stage.

The Ground Rules

Club Guidelines

To keep the club welcoming and fun for everyone, all members agree to follow these guidelines.

1. Respect Everyone

Treat every member, instrument, and idea with respect. All skill levels and music tastes are welcome here.

2. Show Up & Take Part

Try to attend meetings regularly and join in. The club is best when everyone gets involved.

3. Share the Spotlight

Give others a turn to play, lead, and perform. We grow together by lifting each other up.

4. Care for the Space & Gear

Handle shared instruments and equipment carefully, and help clean up after every session.

5. Keep It Positive

Offer kind, helpful feedback. Mistakes are part of learning — no put-downs allowed.

6. Stay Safe

Follow all school rules and keep volume at safe levels. Music should be fun, not harmful.
